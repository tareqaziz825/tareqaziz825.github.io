About me

I am an ICT Assistant at the International Training Network (ITN) Centre, BUET, where I own the institute's digital platforms end to end — the official website and sanitation knowledge repository, two full online courses for government officials, and the data pipelines behind our district-level survey work.

My research background is in computer vision and zero-shot learning. My final-year thesis built a context-aware anomaly detection framework for surveillance video that reached 84.5% AUC without seeing a single anomaly during training.
